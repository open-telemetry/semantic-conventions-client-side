Add the `session` and `app` namespaces, moved together from the core semantic conventions because `app.crash` references `session.id`: the `session.*` and `app.*` attributes, the `session-id` attribute group, the `app` entity and the `session.start`, `session.end`, `app.crash`, `app.screen.click`, `app.widget.click` and `app.jank` events. Core deprecates them in favor of this registry in [open-telemetry/semantic-conventions#4306](https://github.com/open-telemetry/semantic-conventions/pull/4306).
